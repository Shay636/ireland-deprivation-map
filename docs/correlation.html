What it means

Across all 3,420 Electoral Divisions, the Pearson correlation between deprivation score and distance to the nearest listed addiction treatment service is r = -0.179 (R² = 0.032), which is highly statistically significant (p < 0.001). This is a weak negative correlation, meaning that more deprived Electoral Divisions tend to be further from a treatment service. The deprivation score alone explains 3.2% of the variation in service distance.

At the national level this association is partly masked by urban concentration. Ireland's most deprived areas include both inner-city Electoral Divisions — where services are close — and remote rural areas where they are not. These two patterns partially cancel each other out in the national figure.

Restricting the analysis to rural Electoral Divisions (outside the five city council areas, n = 3,071), the correlation strengthens to r = -0.258 (R² = 0.067), still highly statistically significant (p < 0.001), and weak negative. In rural Ireland, more deprived Electoral Divisions tend to be further from a treatment service — without the masking effect of urban service density. The regression slope of -0.47 km per score unit means that for every one-point increase in deprivation (i.e. moving one unit further down the deprivation scale), a rural Electoral Division is on average 0.47 km further from the nearest service.

Bottom line: Yes — in rural Ireland there is a statistically significant association between higher deprivation and greater distance from treatment. The places that need help most are, on average, the hardest to reach.

Method

Deprivation scores: Pobal HP Deprivation Index 2022, relative weight score at Electoral Division level. More negative = more deprived. Distance: straight-line (haversine) from each ED centroid to the nearest of 79 listed addiction treatment services (Dec 2025). Pearson correlation and ordinary least squares regression. Rural EDs defined as those outside the five city council areas (Dublin City, Cork City, Limerick City, Waterford City, Galway City). Analysis excludes EDs with missing deprivation data (n = 0). All computations in Python using SciPy and NumPy.

Service data audit: 79 vs 86

An earlier run of the pipeline included 86 service markers after merging 79 curated services with 7 results from an OpenStreetMap Overpass query. The OSM query was subsequently removed because all 7 results were found to be irrelevant. Each was investigated and classified below. No geocoding was needed: all 7 had valid coordinates but were the wrong type of service.
